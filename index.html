    Method & accuracy

    Model used for this map

    Polygon RF. A Random Forest (Earth Engine smileRandomForest, probability mode, 200 trees, minimum leaf size 5). A pixel is flood if its probability is 0.425 or higher. It is trained on the reference flood polygons of AOIs 01, 02, 05 and 06.

    Inputs (11 bands): Sentinel-1 VV, VH and VV−VH for the selected pass and for a pre-event median from the same orbit; the change in VV and VH; elevation, slope and HAND (height above nearest drainage). Output is masked to floodable ground (HAND below the cut-off, not permanent water), and very small patches are dropped.

    Depth is a separate bathtub estimate on the LINZ LiDAR DEM, not part of the Random Forest.

    Dates (Sentinel-1 passes)

    PassUTCOrbitScenesModel

    The model was trained on the 14 Feb pass (orbit 81) only. The other dates use different orbits and viewing angles, so their maps are unvalidated and have no reference polygons. Single-scene passes likely cover only part of Northland, so their totals are not comparable with the event pass. Sentinel-1 passes over Northland on 12 and 13 Feb do not exist, so the build-up to the peak cannot be shown.

    Accuracy on held-out test AOIs 03 and 04 (event pass)

    Scores come from the scikit-learn version of the Polygon RF used for tuning. The Earth Engine version that made the map scored almost the same (test average precision 0.954 vs 0.952). WorldCover RF and Otsu are baselines for comparison only; they are not shown on this map.

    Read with care. The reference polygons were digitised by the team on the same Sentinel-1 pass, with the SWA Cyclone Gabrielle Impact Map (Sentinel-2) as a guide, and are not field-verified, so these scores measure agreement with the team's interpretation, not independent accuracy. SAR under-detects flooding in built-up areas and under vegetation. Population is WorldPop 2020 (approx.); buildings and roads are LINZ. Depth is a bathtub estimate on the LINZ LiDAR DEM.

    Priority rules. Critical: school or named critical use, or ≥ 10 buildings, or ≥ 25 people. High: ≥ 3 buildings, ≥ 5 people or ≥ 1 km road. Moderate: any building or road. Low: none. Status changes are saved in this browser only.
